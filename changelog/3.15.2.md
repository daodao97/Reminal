# 3.15.2

Fixed
- Opening one session after another could leave you looking at a blank
  screen that never filled in. The PIN from the session you came from was
  being sent to the new one, which cannot work — and it also skipped the
  PIN-free connect that would have.
- A wrong PIN said so for a few seconds and then changed its mind, showing
  a connection timeout instead. It now keeps telling you the PIN is the
  problem, straight away.
- The Share panel could hand out the right session with the wrong PIN, so
  the link you copied and sent to someone could not open anything. It now
  shows the PIN of the session you are actually on.
- Connecting asked the machine to do the handshake twice every time. A
  machine only answers so many in a row before making the next one wait, so
  reconnecting a few times — changing networks, reopening a tab — could
  leave you waiting with nothing on screen.
