# 3.14.29

Fixed
- Opening one of your own sessions from a phone could say "this device
  isn't an owner of this machine" and ask for a PIN, on a machine you
  own and had connected to many times. Nothing was wrong with your
  device: the machine had simply run out of its allowance for answering
  those checks, and stayed silent, which the page read as a refusal.
  Reconnecting a few times — a phone changing networks, a tab reopening
  — was enough to trigger it, and it could last minutes.
- Your devices now get their own, far wider allowance, and proving you
  are the owner costs nothing at all, so no amount of reconnecting can
  lock you out of your own machine.
- When a machine is genuinely too busy for a moment, it now says so and
  the page waits and tries again, instead of telling you that you do not
  own it. A busy machine also no longer keeps you waiting behind whatever
  else it was doing.
