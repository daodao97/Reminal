# 3.14.27

Fixed
- Switching from one session to another in quick succession, for example
  two taps landing on the same spot, could leave the viewer stuck with
  "Handshake failed: Failed to execute 'send' on 'WebSocket'" or connected
  with no working session. The first connection's handshake was finishing
  on the second connection's socket. Each handshake now stays on the
  socket it started on and steps aside when a newer connection takes over.
- A session's name is cleaned of control characters wherever it is set,
  not only when it comes from the terminal's title.
