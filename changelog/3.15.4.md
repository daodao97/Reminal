# 3.15.4

Fixed
- Typing into a window you are viewing from your phone or another computer
  is fast now. Each letter used to be handed to the Mac one at a time in a
  way that took around a twentieth of a second each, so anything faster
  than slow typing fell behind and words arrived seconds after you typed
  them, even though the picture kept updating normally.
- Quitting a coding agent just after your sessions came back from a restart
  now keeps it closed. It could come back on the next restart, because the
  session was still recorded as running it.
