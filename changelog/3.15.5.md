# 3.15.5

Added
- You can name a port you share. `reminal expose 8080 --name "Quarterly
  report"` shows that name in your session list and in the Machines panel
  on the web, instead of "port :8080". The port number is still shown
  beside it, so you can tell two of them apart. With several things shared
  at once, the name is what tells you which is which.
