Fixed
- Window notes no longer disappear on their own. They now survive reminal restarting or upgrading, the window being minimised, hidden, moved to another Space, or the screen locking, and the badge helper crashing. Only closing the window clears them.

- `reminal doctor` now shows how many notes are kept and where.
